# Thailand: chips and geopolitics notes (checked 2026-11-12)

H = primary or multiple sources; M = one secondary source. OBON and Siam AI claims are allegations.

## US chip rules
- Thailand was **Tier 2** under the Jan 2025 AI Diffusion Rule, which was rescinded in May 2025 (project `data/chip_tiers.csv`, H).
- **Jul 2025 draft:** Bloomberg reported a preliminary rule requiring US licences for AI GPUs sent to Malaysia and Thailand ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/gpus/us-reportedly-plans-to-curb-sales-of-ai-gpus-to-malaysia-and-thailand-to-prevent-smuggling-to-china), H). The Next Web, citing Bloomberg, reported on 7 Aug 2026 that Commerce **shelved** it ([TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/us-bis-review-china-remote-offshore-nvidia-chip-access), M). No Thai-specific rule was found in force.
- Under the 31 May 2026 BIS guidance, any buyer with a parent company in China needs a licence, wherever the buyer is based ([Asia Times](https://asiatimes.com/2026/06/nvidia-gpu-crackdown-hits-china-linked-southeast-asia-data-centers/), H).

## Thai controls
- In May 2026 the BOI and Customs cut incentives for "pass-through" assembly and began checking 100% of electronics trade ([Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/business/trade/40066114), M). From 1 Jun 2026 the joint watch list with US CBP grew to 67 items ([Thairath](https://en.thairath.co.th/news/governmentpolicy/2953377), M).
- **Dual-use (TCWMD Act):** the control list is based on the EU's, but licensing covers only Category 0 so far (from 30 Jul 2026). No source confirms that AI chips are licensed ([A&M](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/thought-leadership/thailand-s-export-control-regime-are-your-shipments-ready), M).
- **Data centre pause:** on 4 Sep 2026 Thailand paused 49 projects under construction and 117 awaiting permits over power, water and siting ([ANN](https://asianews.network/?p=299298), H). A seven-tier licensing scheme was due at Cabinet on 10 Nov 2026 ([Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/40073173), M).

## Enforcement
- **Supermicro case (indicted Mar 2026):** prosecutors charged co-founder Wally Liaw, Steven Chang and broker Willy Sun. They allege about $2.5bn of Nvidia servers moved via a Southeast Asian "Company-1" in 2024 and 2025. Bloomberg's sources name Company-1 as **OBON Corp**, Bangkok. OBON has not been charged and Alibaba denies any link ([TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/us-prosecutors-thailand-obon-corp-supermicro-nvidia-alibaba-smuggling), H).
- **Siam AI:** OBON said in May 2024 that it created Siam AI. CEO Ratanaphon Wongnapachant, Thaksin's nephew, ran OBON until at least May 2024. Siam AI is Thailand's first **Nvidia Cloud Partner**, and Jensen Huang attended its event in Dec 2024. Siam AI denies exporting to China and says it is independent of OBON ([The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/05/10/us-learnt-to-suspect-nvidia-chips-were-smuggled-to-alibaba-via-thailand), H).

## US and Chinese stacks
- **US:** AWS opened a region in Jan 2025 with 3 AZs and more than $5bn committed ([Amazon](https://press.aboutamazon.com/2025/1/aws-launches-infrastructure-region-in-thailand), H). Google opened its Bangkok region in Jan 2026 as part of $1bn (H). Microsoft has pledged more than $1bn over two years and has no region live ([Peninsula](https://www.thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/31/03/2026/microsoft-plans-over-1-bn-cloud-ai-investment-in-thailand), M).
- **China:** Alibaba Cloud has 2 data centres (2022 and 2025), and Tencent has 2 Bangkok AZs ([TechWire](https://techwireasia.com/2025/02/alibaba-cloud-opens-second-data-centre-in-thailand-to-drive-digital-innovation/), H). Huawei Cloud has 3 AZs and ranks third in Thai IaaS by revenue (Huawei release, Jul 2026, M). TikTok won BOI approval for THB842bn across 3 sites ([Asia Tech Review](https://www.asiatechreview.com/p/tiktok-doubles-down-on-southeast), H).
- **EEC context:** the BOI has approved THB137.7bn for the EV supply chain (BYD, Changan, GAC Aion, GWM and MG) (The Star, Aug 2025, H). Chinese firms make up 17% of industrial-estate investors, up from 6% in 2019 ([Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/business/40067791), M).

## Thailand and the US
- Thailand faced a 36% IEEPA tariff from Apr 2025, then 19% under the Oct 2025 framework. After the Supreme Court ruling, a 10% Section 122 tariff applied from 24 Feb to 24 Jul 2026. Since then a **12.5% Section 301** tariff over forced labour has applied, and an excess-capacity probe is pending ([Tilleke](https://www.tilleke.com/insights/business-impact-of-new-us-section-301-tariffs-on-thailand), H).
- On 5 Nov 2026 the deal was agreed in principle, with an expected rate of "19% or lower." That is above today's 12.5%, and the sources do not explain why ([Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/business/40073136), M).

## Thailand and China
- Two-way trade was $147.8bn in 2025, and China is Thailand's "most important trading partner" ([The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/06/11/chinas-opening-up-drive-gives-thai-exporters-new-momentum), H). The deficit with China was $60.6bn in Jan to Nov 2025.
- High-speed rail phase 1 (250.77 km) was 52.4% complete in Mar 2026, and the 2030 target is at risk (The Star, H).
- No Thai data-localisation rule aimed at China was found.

## Politics
PM Paetongtarn was removed on 29 Aug 2025, and Anutin was elected on 5 Sep 2025 ([CRS](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/IN12587.html), H). After a February 2026 election, Anutin was re-elected on 19 Mar 2026 at the head of a Bhumjaithai and Pheu Thai coalition (H).
