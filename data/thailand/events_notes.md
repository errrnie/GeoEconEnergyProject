# Thailand events: notes

As of 12 Nov 2026. 45 events: Investment 25, Energy and water 8, Thailand policy 6, Geopolitics 6.

## Method
- Every row links a page that was fetched and read. Order of preference: BOI, company and BIS releases, then The Nation, Bloomberg/Reuters (via The Star, w.media), Fulcrum and The Next Web. Pack v1 items were re-fetched before use.
- `amount_usd_m` is filled only where the source gave USD. For BOI rounds it is the data centre or digital total.
- `mw` is the MW named in the event. The 16 Sep 2026 row is requested load, not capacity.
- Month precision: Alibaba (May 2022), BOI Google/GDS (Nov 2024), Stratus (Jun 2025), Super Micro indictment (Mar 2026).
