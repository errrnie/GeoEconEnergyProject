# Indonesia data notes (built 12 Nov 2026)

## Method
- Each row cites one URL opened in this session. Primary sources come first (company releases, Setkab, Singapore MFA and EMA, BIS, White House, Oracle docs), then Jakarta Post, Kontan, Katadata, DCD, W.Media and others. Baxtel and datacentermap are used only for placement and conflicts.
- No coordinates are site-level. All are `town` points: district or city centres from Wikipedia (Nongsa, Central Cikarang, Cibitung, Purwakarta) or bamwor.com (Surakarta). Sites in Bekasi city, Karawang and the Jakarta CBD have no coordinates.
- Undisclosed MW is left blank. Capex is the headline pledge, and notes flag where it includes opex, skills spending or other countries.
- Megaspeed and the INF Tech/Indosat GPU rental are written as reports. Megaspeed's Indonesian link is Low confidence because it rests on a TechFlow/KuCoin summary of Bloomberg and NYT.

## Top 8 key moments
1. 20 Mar 2018: Nongsa Digital Park opens in Batam.
2. Oct 2019: GR 71/2019 narrows data localisation to public-scope operators.
3. 24 Jun 2020: Google Jakarta goes live, the first hyperscale region (AWS follows Dec 2021).
4. 20 Jun 2024: Ransomware cripples the national data centre.
5. 13 Jan 2025: The US Diffusion Rule puts Indonesia in Tier 2. It is rescinded 13 May.
6. 22 Jul 2025: The US trade framework allows personal data transfers to the US.
7. 17 Apr 2026: DayOne signs a 450MW PPA with PLN Batam. Firmus and Nvidia follow in June with a 360MW AI factory.
8. 26 Sep to 5 Oct 2026: Batam water protests target data centres.

## Conflicts
- **National capacity.** PLN reports about 580MW of IT installed in 2026. The energy ministry says about 1.1GW of demand in 2025 and 5.2GW by 2034. PLN puts requests at 25GW by 2034. These measure different things.
- **BW Digital NDP-1.** 144MW, 120MW or 24MW, depending on the source.
- **DCI H2 Karawang.** 791ha (DCD) against 86ha (The Tech Capital).
- **DayOne Kabil.** 450MW of grid supply against 240MW of IT (Baxtel).
- **SM+ SMX01.** Planned at 18MW, but 21MW at opening.
- **PDN Cikarang.** Handed over in Apr 2025. Headlines say it ran only in limited form by Jan 2026.
- **Possible double counts.** Firmus with DayOne Kabil, and NeutraDC's 200MW MoU against its 400MW target.

## Gaps
- Hyperscalers disclose no sites or MW.
- There is no evidence of construction at the Indosat-Nvidia Solo centre.
- Missing: Tencent's and Alibaba's early opening dates, EdgeConneX and STT live MW, Bridge Data Centres, Equinix JK2, and BKPM approval data.
- BP Batam's count of 16 hyperscale projects is not reconciled site by site.

## Plain history
Indonesia's market started with local operators such as DCI Indonesia serving banks and government in Jakarta, under rules that kept public-service data onshore. Chinese clouds came early: Alibaba built its first local data centre in 2018, followed by Tencent and Huawei. Google (2020) and AWS (2021) then opened Jakarta regions, drawn by the size of the market. GR 71/2019 let private firms host offshore, but public and financial data stayed local, so demand held. Wholesale campuses moved east to the Bekasi, Cibitung and Cikarang industrial estates, where land and substations were available. Meanwhile Batam's Nongsa Digital Park, helped by its own utility and new subsea cables, became Singapore's second overflow after Johor. From 2024, AI pledges grew to billions (Microsoft, Edgnex, Digital Edge, Firmus with Nvidia), as Jakarta joined BRICS, struck a US trade deal and faced shifting chip controls. By late 2026 the real limits were power connections on Java and water in Batam.
