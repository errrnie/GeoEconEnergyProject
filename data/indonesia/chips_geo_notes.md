# Indonesia: chips and geopolitics notes (checked 2026-11-12)

H = primary or multiple sources; M = one secondary source. Megaspeed claims are allegations.

## US chip rules
- Indonesia was **Tier 2** under the Jan 2025 AI Diffusion Rule, which was rescinded in May 2025 (project `data/shared/chip_tiers.csv`, H).
- **Now:** Indonesia is not in D:1, D:4 or D:5, so no country licence is needed for AI chips ([15 CFR 740 Supp. 1](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/15/appendix-Supplement_No_1_to_part_740), M). The 31 May 2026 parent-company guidance and end-use controls still apply. No Indonesia-specific draft rule was found, unlike Malaysia and Thailand.

## Enforcement (allegations)
- **Megaspeed:** Bloomberg's analysis of customs records says Singapore-based Megaspeed's affiliates imported at least **$4.6bn** of Nvidia hardware (136,000+ GPUs) in 2023-25 ([Tech Times, 10 Nov 2026](https://www.techtimes.co.uk/megaspeed-nvidia-hardware-imports-chip-scrutiny-1809823), M).
  - The affiliates are **PT Megaspeed International Indonesia** and Speedmatrix (Malaysia), and the figure is not split between them.
  - A US criminal inquiry and a Singapore police probe are reported as ongoing.
  - Megaspeed denies diversion. Nvidia calls the claims "debunked", but its board opened a review on 8 Nov 2026.
- No Indonesian case or export-control action on AI chips was found.

## US and Chinese stacks
- **US:** Google (2020), AWS (2021, about $5bn over 15 years) and Microsoft (2025, $1.7bn pledge) run Jakarta regions (project `data/region/timeline.csv`, H).
- **China:** Tencent has 2 Jakarta data centres and is adding a third with $500m by 2030 ([DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/tencent-to-invest-500m-for-data-center-development-in-indonesia), M). Alibaba and Huawei clouds also operate in Jakarta (project `countries.csv`, M).

## Chinese investment
- **Nickel:** Chinese firms control about **75%** of nickel refining capacity ([Mining Technology/C4ADS, Feb 2025](https://www.mining-technology.com/news/chinas-indonesias-nickel-capacity), M), and the smelters run on 15.4 GW of captive coal.
- **CATL:** broke ground in Jun 2025 on a **$6bn** chain with Antam and IBC, from Halmahera nickel to a 6.9 GWh cell plant in Karawang ([Energy-Storage.news](https://www.energy-storage.news/catl-begins-us6-billion-complete-value-chain-battery-integration-project-in-indonesia/), H).

## BRICS
Indonesia became a **full member on 6 Jan 2025** ([Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/world/2025/01/07/indonesia-joins-brics-bloc-as-full-member), H).

## US tariffs
- **Feb 2026:** the Agreement on Reciprocal Trade (19% rate) was signed on **19 Feb**. On **20 Feb** the Supreme Court ruled that IEEPA does not authorise tariffs ([DFDL, 24 Aug 2026](https://www.dfdl.com/insights/legal-and-tax-updates/us-indonesia-trade-deal/), H).
- **Feb to Jul 2026:** a Section 122 surcharge of 10% (flagged to rise to 15%) applied until 24 Jul ([CSIS Indonesia in The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/business/insight/2026/02/26/after-the-court-ruling-whats-next-for-the-indonesia-us-trade-deal), M).
- **Since 24 Jul:** a **10% Section 301** duty applies. Thailand's rate is 12.5%.
- **ART status:** it is **not in force**, and DFDL calls the 19% "politically, not legally, settled". The sources conflict on whether any 19% layer is collected.
- **ART terms:** it lifts US-bound export limits on nickel, cobalt, bauxite and tin, and permits commercial data transfers (state data excluded).

## Danantara and AI
- **Fund:** Danantara was launched on 24 Feb 2025 with $20bn and a target of more than $900bn in assets ([Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/business/2025/02/24/indonesia-launches-sovereign-wealth-fund-aiming-to-hold-900-bn-in-assets.html), H).
- **Data centres:** the CTO said on 19 Oct 2026 that the push is worth over **Rp30tn (~$1.7bn)**, with about 1 GW in the pipeline. Partners include Arm, and chip queues run 16-48 months ([Okezone via RCTI+](https://www.rctiplus.com/news/detail/okezone/5497502/danantara-bidik-sektor-data-center-rp30-triliun--bukan-sekadar-sewa-lahan), M).
- **Coal risk:** CIO Pandu Sjahrir wrote that coal "risks limiting" the AI opportunity ([Fortune, Apr 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/04/11/indonesia-danantara-cio-ai-energy-economy-governance-pandu-sjahrir/), M).
