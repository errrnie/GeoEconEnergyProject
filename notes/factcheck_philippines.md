## Confirmed as written
- BMI ~140 MW live, 44 facilities, 43 MW UC, 221 MW planned (TNGlobal, 28 Sep 2026).
- JLL Manila 73.6 MW operating, 157 MW under development (JLL Q2 2025).
- DICT 18 GW over the next decade; "nearly 500 MW" is DCPH members' combined figure (Manila Bulletin, 25 Feb 2026).
- Masterplan US$34.4bn, 1.5 GW by 2033, ~400 MW by 2030, 50 MW baseline, Clark-Bataan primary (W.Media, 15 Sep 2026).
- Meralco 30+ applications totalling 1,200 MW (BusinessWorld, May 2024).
- DOE 2024: coal 79,359 of 126,941 GWh = 62.5%; 2022 59.6%; Luzon peak 12,550 to 14,016 MW (+11.7%) (DOE 2024 Power Statistics Summary).
- First LNG cargo April 2023 (Manila Bulletin, 16 Apr 2023).
- Red alert 16 Apr 2024, 19 plants on forced outage, rotational brownouts (PNA).
- World Bank residential prices: PH ~0.21, SG 0.23, TH 0.13, ID 0.09, MY 0.05 US$/kWh (GMA, 6 Aug 2026), so "second only to Singapore" holds for that comparison.
- State Grid 40% of NGCP; Maharlika would want it "if it becomes available" (BusinessWorld, 29 Jan 2025).
- Huawei Cloud 3-AZ Philippines Region, 3 Dec 2024 (Huawei release; DCD).
- AWS Manila Local Zone GA June 2023; AWS region only being explored (up to US$5bn over 15 years, Marcos meeting 8 Jul 2026, Context.ph). No Google or Microsoft PH region found.
- Equinix completed purchase of TIM's MN1-MN3, June 2025 (Equinix release).
- Pax Silica signed 17 Apr 2026 by Usec. Rodolfo, 13th signatory (The Star/Bernama).
- Section 301 12.5% from 24 Jul 2026; DFA rejected forced-labour findings.
- Tier 2 in Jan 2025 AI Diffusion Rule; rescinded May 2025; no country licence today (site-wide facts).
- WorldRiskIndex 2025: Philippines ranked first (Manila Bulletin, 26 Sep 2025).
- GEA-4: 123 projects, 10,195 MW, mostly solar, ~1.2 GW with storage (Enerdata, TaiyangNews).
- RA 11659 (21 Mar 2022) and DOE DC2022-11-0034 (Nov 2022) ownership changes (PNA, ASEAN Briefing).
- Site coordinates: all town-level points sit in the right towns (spot-checked all 20).

## Not independently verified
- C&W build cost US$8.2m/MW (Malaysia 9.6): the C&W 2026 cost guide figures are images; values kept as in data/shared/countries.csv.
- Actual 2024 LNG import volume: Energy Aspects' 2025 forecast implies about 1.4 Mt for 2024, above GIIGNL's 1.17 Mt expectation. Kept 1.17 Mt but labelled as expected.
