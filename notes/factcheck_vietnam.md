## Confirmed
- Telecom Law 24/2023/QH15 passed 24 Nov 2023; 100% foreign ownership in data centre and cloud from 1 Jan 2025 (UNCTAD).
- Viettel 140 MW Tan Phu Trung groundbreaking 23 Apr 2025, first above 100 MW (The Investor, VnEconomy, VietNamNet); Hoa Lac 30 MW opened Apr 2024 (The Investor).
- AWS Local Zone Hanoi GA 19 Jun 2026 (AWS); zone ID ap-southeast-1-han-1a, Singapore parent.
- Evolution 52 MW IT, US$508.7m; Starmason 60 MW IT, US$480.2m; licensed 25 Apr 2026 at Saigon Hi-Tech Park.
- EVN 2024 coal 49.5%, hydro 28.7% (arithmetic checked against energy.csv GWh; EVN AR 2024-2025). 2024 sales +9.08%, about 23 TWh.
- 2023 shortage 900 GWh and World Bank US$1.4bn (~0.3% GDP) (VnExpress via energy.csv).
- 500 kV circuit 3: 5,000 MW, double the 2,500 MW limit (EVN).
- Nhon Trach 3 and 4 total 1,624 MW.
- Vietnam in D1 (and D3); Oct 2023 advanced computing rule, in force 17 Nov 2023, licence requirement for D1 (The Investor, 21 Oct 2023).
- C4ADS "Covert Compute", 9 Sep 2026: 50 shipments, about US$13.4m, via Vietnam, India and Malaysia to Hong Kong and China, 2023 to 2025; no Vietnamese firm named.
- Section 301 12.5% on Vietnam effective 24 Jul 2026 (VnEconomy). Supreme Court IEEPA ruling 20 Feb 2026.
- Cushman & Wakefield 2026 cost guide mid figures: Vietnam US$7.2m/MW, Malaysia 9.6, Thailand 8.8, Singapore 14.4 (C&W Singapore release, 1 Apr 2026). Hanoi + HCMC ~73 MW operating, 137 MW more by 2030 (Tuoi Tre, 7 Apr 2026).
- 870 MW 2030 target from a Ministry of Science and Technology report, tied to the ICT infrastructure master plan (The Investor, Apr 2025).
- Savills 524.7 MW (2025) and ~950 MW (2030) (VietnamPlus, 28 Aug 2026).
- Viettel first DGX B200, Feb 2026 (Tuoi Tre). STT GDC exit, lease to 2041 (Baxtel, Aug 2026).
- Scenario numbers internally consistent (share of 2024 generation, gas and LNG conversions); neighbours' high cases 7.35 Mt (Malaysia) and 9.04 Mt (Thailand) match "about 7" and "about 9".
- Park-centre coordinates look right for Saigon Hi-Tech Park, Tan Thuan, Hoa Lac and Da Nang Hi-Tech Park.
