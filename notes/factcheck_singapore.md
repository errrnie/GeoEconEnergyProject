## Summary (problems only)

Wrong: "most operating capacity in Southeast Asia" (the page's own chart has Malaysia at 1.29 GW vs 1.04 GW), and tranches "since 2019" (2019 to 2022 was a pause). The page uses three Singapore capacity figures (1.46, 1.12, 1.04 GW), and the BMI vs Knight Frank funnel contradicts "drew level". "Largest market" depends on the source. The 7% share is a 2020 figure; 2024 estimates exceed 10%. Stale: the fraud case (a fourth person, companies and laundering charges were added in 2026) and Nvidia's 18% (it no longer reports a Singapore line). Unsourced: Megaspeed's "largest operation in Malaysia". Minor: A:6 wording, the missing Aug 2026 remote-access draft, green-fuel conditions applied to 2023, "all" power fuel imported, coal missing from the legend, no attribution for the 3 Mt gap, 95% vs 93% in an event, the DayOne event date, and STT GDC ownership.
