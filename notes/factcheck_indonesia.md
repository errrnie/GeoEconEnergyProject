## Confirmed

- ~580 MW DC IT capacity 2026, 3.5 GW by 2030, 25.3 GW by 2034, 99% on JAMALI (Kontan, 23 Sep 2026, PLN EVP Nayusrizal).
- 5.2 GW energy ministry 2034 forecast (Investortrust headline); IDPRO 11 GW by 2030 (Kabar Bursa, 5 Sep 2026).
- DayOne 511 MVA / ~450 MW PPA with PLN Batam, 17 Apr 2026, phased 2026-27 (DayOne release). PLN Batam 844 MW capability (Ombudsman Kepri, Dec 2025).
- Firmus, DayOne and Nvidia 360 MW Batam AI factory, planned, 29 Jun 2026 (W.Media).
- Nongsa blockade 5 Oct 2026, ~53% supply restored (The Star / Jakarta Post, 8 Oct 2026).
- Batam water 382 ML/d, Duriangkang 80%, DC need ~32 ML/d by 2032 = 8.4% (Katadata, A Digital Boom in a Thirsty City).
- Java rolling blackouts 20 Jun 2026; RUPTL adds no non-renewable capacity on JAMALI 2030-34 (Katadata, 6 Jul 2026).
- Malaysia 2025 coal imports 36.3 Mt, 27.1 Mt (~75%) from Indonesia (Argus).
- Chinese firms ~75% of Indonesian nickel refining capacity (C4ADS via Mining.com, Feb 2025).
- ART signed 19 Feb 2026, not in force; Section 301 10% from 24 Jul 2026 (DFDL).
- Key-moment timeline rows (2018-03-20 Nongsa, Oct 2019 GR 71, 24 Jun 2020 Google, 20 Jun 2024 ransomware, 13 Jan 2025 Tier 2, 22 Jul 2025 framework 32% to 19% with data transfer clause, 17 Apr 2026 DayOne, 5 Oct 2026 blockade) match their cited sources.
- Megaspeed wording matches site-wide settled facts (US$4.6bn, allegations, nobody charged).
