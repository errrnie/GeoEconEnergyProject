# Singapore: why new capacity is scarce

- **Pause, 2019 to 2022.** Singapore stopped approving new data centres in 2019; the moratorium was lifted in January 2022 ([Computer Weekly](https://www.computerweekly.com/news/252522992/Singapore-to-restart-datacentre-builds-with-sustainability-focus)). EDB describes it as a "temporary pause" lifted in 2022. Confidence: high.
- **DC-CFA pilot (2023): about 80 MW.** Awarded 14 Jul 2023 to Equinix, GDS, Microsoft and an AirTrunk-ByteDance consortium ([EDB](https://www.edb.gov.sg/en/about-edb/media-releases-publications/four-data-centre-proposals-selected-as-part-of-pilot-data-centre-call-for-application.html)). High.
- **"At least 300 MW" plan (30 May 2024).** Green Data Centre Roadmap aims to add at least 300 MW in the near term, on an existing base of about 1.4 GW, with more possible via green energy ([Linklaters](https://techinsights.linklaters.com/post/102j94y/powering-up-to-go-green-singapore-announces-at-least-300-megawatts-increase-in-d)). High.
- **DC-CFA2: 200 MW.** Launched 1 Dec 2025 offering at least 200 MW ([EDB](https://www.edb.gov.sg/en/news-and-insights/launch-of-second-data-centre-call-for-application)). On 21 Aug 2026, 50 MW each went to Digital Realty, Equinix, Keppel DC and STT GDC, all on Jurong Island, each committing to source over 50% green energy; 20 proposals competed. Another round to be reviewed in 18 to 24 months ([EDB](https://www.edb.gov.sg/en/news-and-insights/four-data-centre-proposals-selected-as-part-of-second-data-centre); [w.media](https://w.media/singapores-dc-cfa2-winners-announced-third-cfa-possible/)). High.
- **Power.** Data centres use over 7% of Singapore's electricity ([King & Wood Mallesons, Dec 2025](https://www.mallesons.com/sg/en/insights/latest-thinking/singapore-launches-200mw-data-centre-call-for-application-dc-cfa2)). CBRE (Jul 2026): power availability is "a big bottleneck", inventory limited through 2026 ([w.media](https://w.media/special-report-hyperscale-ai-drive-apacs-dc-growth-amid-power-regulatory-constraints-cbre/)). High.
- **Land.** "Finite space for data centres amid competing land uses"; DC-CFA2 capacity was steered to a JTC park on Jurong Island ([KWM](https://www.mallesons.com/sg/en/insights/latest-thinking/singapore-launches-200mw-data-centre-call-for-application-dc-cfa2)). High.
- **Scale.** Allocations since 2022 total about 280 MW (80 + 200) against roughly 1.0 to 1.1 GW operating (C&W: 1,043 MW end-2025; Knight Frank: 1,118 MW mid-2026). Johor added about 700 MW of operating capacity in 2025 to H1 2026 alone (C&W: 401 to 1,110 MW).
