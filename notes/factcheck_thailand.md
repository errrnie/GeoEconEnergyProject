## Summary of problems

1. Allegations: "OBON created Siam AI" is OBON's own claim, stated as fact. The indictment says "Southeast Asia", not Bangkok. Siam AI's denial is overstated.
2. The 4.5m baht/MW bond was approved by the NEPC on 15 Jul, not "proposed".
3. "Raised the price of power" overstates: no rate is set.
4. The reserve margin is 51%, not 55%.
5. The 8.8 GW is a high-growth case with no year. The 2.87 GW has no dates.
6. The funnel note credits the wrong sources.
7. LNG is about 3x Malaysia's in the same year, not 5x.
8. Gulf output recovered after the Erawan handover. The 58% gas share is stale.
9. The stacks h2 misleads, and STT's tenant is disclosed.
10. Timeline: Alibaba was not the second Chinese cloud, "approvals" should be "applications", and the tier caps never bound.
11. The global chart's Thailand bar is stale.
