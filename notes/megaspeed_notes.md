# Megaspeed International: what is publicly reported (as of 11 Nov 2026)

**Relevance to Malaysia: direct.** Megaspeed's main operating arm is a Malaysian company, Speedmatrix Sdn Bhd. Its biggest known site was a Malaysian data centre, and the chip import figures come from Malaysian customs records. US officials inspected its hardware in Malaysia.

Everything below is reported allegation or company statement. No court or regulator has found Megaspeed guilty of anything.

## Who it is
- Megaspeed International Pte Ltd is a Singapore "neocloud" that rents GPU computing. It started in 2015 as **7Road International**, the overseas arm of Chinese game developer 7Road, and was renamed Megaspeed in March 2023. Ownership later moved through a BVI entity and then to a Singapore company owned by CEO Tan Yong Pong ([Thairath, citing Bloomberg](https://www.thairath.co.th/money/tech_innovation/tech_companies/2903761); [TechTimes UK](https://www.techtimes.co.uk/megaspeed-nvidia-hardware-imports-chip-scrutiny-1809823)).
- Sen. Tom Cotton's letter of 13 Oct 2025 calls it a spin-off of a "Chinese government-owned" firm. It says most executives are based in China, including CEO "James Tan", a Singaporean living in Shanghai ([Cotton](https://www.cotton.senate.gov/news/press-releases/cotton-to-lutnick-investigate-chinese-government-spin-off-megaspeed-for-skirting-us-export-laws)). *Unclear whether "James Tan" and Tan Yong Pong are the same person.*
- Former executive Alice Huang (Huang Le) was director and managing director from Mar to Nov 2023, per The Business Times via TechTimes UK. US officials reportedly believe she worked for Chinese intelligence. Beijing calls this unsubstantiated, and it is unproven ([TNW, citing NYT](https://thenextweb.com/news/megaspeed-probe-commerce-nvidia-lawyer-justice-department-nyt)).

## Malaysia footprint
- **Speedmatrix Sdn Bhd** ran a data centre in Sedenak Tech Park, Johor, and had an office in a Johor mall. It received over 200 shipments of restricted Nvidia hardware between Jun 2024 and Jun 2025, worth nearly US$2B. Most came via a US subsidiary of Inspur ([The Edge Malaysia, citing NYT, 10 Oct 2025](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/773583)).
- A US Commerce official visited the Malaysian site in late 2024 and found some Nvidia equipment still boxed (same sources; [Cotton letter](https://www.cotton.senate.gov/news/press-releases/cotton-to-lutnick-investigate-chinese-government-spin-off-megaspeed-for-skirting-us-export-laws)).
- Bain Capital's **Bridge Data Centres** hosted Megaspeed's largest site, in Malaysia. In Feb 2026 Bridge replaced it with Zenlayer for all 68.4MW ([FMT/Bloomberg](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/business/2026/04/08/bain-capitals-data-centre-unit-cuts-ties-with-nvidia-buyer-after-us-probe)). *Unclear whether this is the same Sedenak facility.*
- Bloomberg reports that Alibaba used Nvidia chips in Malaysia through Megaspeed, via a Singapore shell ultimately owned by Alibaba ([TNW, 7 Aug 2026](https://thenextweb.com/news/us-bis-review-china-remote-offshore-nvidia-chip-access)).

## Elsewhere
- **Indonesia:** PT Megaspeed International Indonesia imports and data centres are reported (Thairath/Bloomberg).
- **Thailand:** I found no reporting of Megaspeed operations there.

## Scale
- At least **US$4.6B and about 136,000 Nvidia GPUs** were imported by the Malaysian and Indonesian units from 2023 to Nov 2025, more than half of them Blackwell (Bloomberg, Dec 2025, via Thairath).
- **17,544 GB300s (about US$787M)** were imported into Malaysia in Nov 2025. Nvidia says it banned sales to Megaspeed in June 2025. Nvidia's count to US officials was about 50,000 units below Bloomberg's ([TNW, citing Bloomberg, 9 Nov 2026](https://thenextweb.com/news/nvidia-megaspeed-ban-gb300-imports-bloomberg)).
- Fortune cites NYT for "more than US$4.8B" over two years ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/10/2-scandals-1-destination-why-nvidia-cant-shake-china-smuggling-allegations/)). The figures differ by source and period.

## Investigations (status Nov 2026)
- **US Commerce (BIS):** The probe was paused in late 2025 at the DOJ's request, after Nvidia's general counsel contacted agents. The DOJ reportedly runs a criminal inquiry. Megaspeed is **not** on the Entity List ([TNW, citing NYT](https://thenextweb.com/news/megaspeed-probe-commerce-nvidia-lawyer-justice-department-nyt)).
- **Singapore:** Megaspeed says Singapore closed its case with no adverse action. Singapore's Home Affairs ministry and police said on 31 Oct 2026 that the probe is ongoing (TechTimes UK). Alice Huang's own case over false import declarations was reportedly closed for lack of evidence.
- **Malaysia:** Megaspeed says (23 Dec 2025) that US and Malaysian inspections found no illegal transfers. I found no Malaysian enforcement action against Speedmatrix.
- **Nvidia:** Nvidia says the diversion claims were "investigated and debunked". Megaspeed remained a listed Nvidia Cloud Partner as of 10 Nov 2026. Nvidia's board opened an independent review on 8 Nov 2026.
- **Congress:** Cotton letter (Oct 2025), Warren and Banks pressure, and a House briefing requested for 18 Nov 2026.
