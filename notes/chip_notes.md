# US AI chip tiers and Malaysia (Nov 2026)

## How tiering worked
The Biden AI Diffusion rule (announced 13 Jan 2025, 90 FR 4544) split the world in three.
- **Tier 1:** US plus 18 allies. Essentially unrestricted.
- **Tier 3:** D:5 arms-embargoed countries plus Macau (China, Russia, Iran and others). Effectively banned, as before.
- **Tier 2:** everyone else, including Malaysia. Countries not listed in chip_tiers.csv were all Tier 2.

Tier 2 countries had a 2025 to 2027 cap of 790 million TPP, about 50,000 H100s (CFR estimate), which could double with security assurances. **Universal VEUs** (Tier 1 firms) could build anywhere but had to keep 75% of compute in Tier 1 and at most 7% in any one other country. **National VEUs** got their own per-country caps, about 320,000 H100s over two years. The **LPP exemption** let buyers outside Tier 3 take about 1,700 H100s a year without counting against caps.

## After rescission
On 13 May 2025 BIS dropped enforcement and promised a replacement. It also issued guidance on Huawei Ascend chips and diversion red flags. That left the October 2023 rules: licences are needed for D:1, D:4 and D:5 countries unless they are also in A:5 or A:6. Malaysia is in none of these groups. Nothing replaced the tiers. A global framework was reported in draft in March 2026 but never issued. The US moved to deals instead: G42/Humain approvals, a UAE upgrade, and EO 14320 on AI exports.

## Malaysia now
- **No US country cap or licence is in force (medium confidence).** BIS's 13 May 2025 release does list "additional export licensing requirements" for Malaysia and Thailand. Law-firm summaries omit this, though, and Bloomberg called it an unfinished draft in July 2025. It was reportedly shelved in Aug 2026, and I found no Federal Register rule.
- **Ownership now matters.** Since 31 May 2026, China- or Macau-parented buyers need a licence anywhere, Johor included.
- **Self-policing:** Malaysia has required Strategic Trade Permits since 14 Jul 2025 and pledged to align with US controls in its Oct 2025 trade deal (ART). Customs seized servers at KLIA in June 2026.
