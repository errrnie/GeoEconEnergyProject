Indonesia's Two Fronts

Indonesia is two data centre markets. Greater Jakarta serves the region's largest population, with US and Chinese cloud regions and rules that keep public data at home, and runs on a coal-heavy grid. Batam, 20 km from Singapore, is becoming Singapore's second overflow after Johor, on a small island grid where water is now the tighter limit. This page follows both.

2 · Why Indonesia

A huge home market and a back door to Singapore

Batam, Singapore's other overflow

Nongsa Digital Park opened in 2018, 20 km from Singapore with its own utility and new subsea cables. In April 2026 DayOne signed a 450 MW power deal with PLN Batam, and Firmus and Nvidia followed with plans for a 360 MW AI factory.

Slow to connect

A 100 MW grid connection takes about 36 months, against the roughly 12 months Malaysia's Green Lane targets. Java's reserve margin is shrinking, and Java had rolling blackouts in June 2026.

DayOne in Batam

The former international arm of China's GDS, now headquartered in Singapore, signed a 450 MW power deal with PLN Batam in April 2026. That one contract is more than half of the island grid's 844 MW capability.

Chinese capital beyond data centres

Chinese firms control about three quarters of Indonesia's nickel refining, and CATL broke ground in 2025 on a US$6 billion battery chain ending in a cell plant in Karawang, the same corridor as Jakarta's data centres.

8 · Leverage and weak points

Forecasts run far ahead of the grid, and Batam runs short of water

Sources: PLN and the energy ministry via Indonesian press, 2025 to 2026; IDPRO industry association; BP Batam and local water data. The forecasts measure different things and are shown for scale.

Weak points

  • On Java the marginal fuel is coal, which makes data centres here among the most carbon-heavy in the region and hard to sell to buyers with clean-power targets.
  • Grid connections take about three years, and Java's spare capacity is shrinking.
  • In Batam one 450 MW deal takes more than half of the island grid, and one reservoir supplies 80% of the island's water.
  • A temporary national data centre was crippled by ransomware in June 2024.

Leverage

  • The largest home market in Southeast Asia, with rules that keep public and much financial data onshore.
  • Its own coal and gas, so it is the least exposed of the region's hosts to imported fuel prices.
  • A new sovereign fund, Danantara, is targeting data centre investment it values at more than US$1.7 billion (Rp30 trillion).
