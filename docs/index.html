How compute came to Southeast Asia

The US and China build most AI infrastructure at home. The overflow is landing in Southeast Asia, first in Singapore, then across the causeway in Johor, and now in Bangkok, Jakarta and beyond. This site follows where it went, why, and what each host country is giving up to get it.
